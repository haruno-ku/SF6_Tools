## Method

1. `tools/lua/pipeline.lua` runs explore.lua's own settings for each of the 31
   characters: modern, midscreen, no counter-hit, at most 3 moves, beam 4000, Drive Rush
   Cancel edges excluded. Each route is reduced to the dozen numbers in
   `practicescore.route_summary`.
2. Every route's `execution_cost` goes into one pooled list; its 25th percentile is
   the cheap-route cut, **6.00**.
3. The five components are computed per character as the tables above describe. Each
   one keeps the count it came from, so it can be checked.
4. Each component's signals are **min-max normalised across the characters present**:
   0 is the worst character in this set on that signal and 1 the best. Route counts are
   log-scaled first. Min-max rather than a z-score because several signals are skewed
   by one or two characters - Guile's 2797 worklist pairs, Dhalsim's 2681 routes - and
   a z-score over a skewed distribution reads as a precise claim about a distribution
   that is not normal. Two consequences: the bottom of every column is 0 by
   construction, and dropping a character moves everyone else.
5. The components are combined at the weights below. A component that could not be
   computed is **dropped and the remaining weights renormalised**, and the character is
   flagged - never scored zero, which would be a claim about the character rather than
   about the data.

## Weights

Tunable in `tools/lua/practicescore.lua`. They are published so a reader can disagree
with them; the component columns above are there so the disagreement can be acted on
without rerunning anything.

| Component | Weight | Why |
|---|---:|---|
| easy damage | 0.30 | A route nobody would use is not practice. A character whose cheap routes already do damage gives a learner something back for the first thing they learn. |
| input shape | 0.25 | What the hands actually do - inputs, motion length, grip changes. The only execution signal here that is counted off the notation rather than guessed. |
| timing comfort | 0.25 | margin_frames and the mechanism are the closest thing to a link window the data has. Equal to input shape because timing and motion are the two halves of execution and nothing here favours one. |
| route availability | 0.10 | Many gauge-free routes means drilling without also learning resource management. Small, because a count of unverified candidates measures the search. |
| data quality | 0.10 | A character whose numbers are shaky should not out-rank one whose numbers hold. Small and last, because the join quality is a property of this repo. |

Inside the components:

- **easy damage**: best cheap scaled damage 0.50, best Pareto damage/cost 0.50.
- **input shape**: mean inputs 0.30, mean longest motion 0.25, 4+ motion share 0.20, mean method switches 0.15, simple-input share 0.10 (the only one where higher is easier).
- **timing comfort**: one signal, the forgiving share.
- **route availability**: one signal, log of the no-gauge route count.
- **data quality**: frame-data join 0.40, confident share 0.40, exact-join share 0.20.

## What this cannot see

- **No measured link windows.** Not one. `margin_frames` is a subtraction over two
  frame-table rows, and `core/Scoring.lua` says it plainly: a one-frame link and a
  six-frame link look identical from here. Everything called "comfortable" above is a
  prediction about a window nobody has swept.
- **Almost nothing has been to the lab.** Of these 31 characters, only Zangief has been to the lab - 7 confirmed combos out of 1000 trials.
  Every other character has zero trials, so there is nothing to calibrate against.
- **Combos are not a character.** Nothing here is about anti-airs, okizeme, movement,
  neutral, defence, resource management, or any matchup. A character that is easy to
  combo with can still be a hard character to play, and the reverse.
- **Modern only.** The pipeline generates modern and excludes Classic rows from
  probing, so a Classic player's answer is not in this table.
- **Beam-truncated searches.** The route search hits its beam limit for most of the
  roster. The routes it did not reach are not in any component, and they are not
  random - the beam keeps what looked good early. This is deliberately the SAME
  settings for every character, including one the pipeline searches deeper for its
  own pages (`all.lua --deep`). A ranking where one character had been searched
  further than the rest would be comparing different populations of routes, so the
  flag beside a name here is about THIS search, not about that character's own
  report - which may well say the search finished.
- **Damage scaling is an unverified model.** `predicted_damage_scaled` comes from
  `core/DamageScaling`, built from public write-ups and never checked against this
  build. It is used for ordering and must not be read as damage.
- **The routes are candidates.** Every route in every component is a sequence the frame
  data says is worth a trial. None of them is known to combo.
- **Min-max is relative.** A 0 in a column means "last of these 31", not "none".
